Our story

From family startup to KW | Innovations.

L&V Civil Contracting started in early 2022 as a family-owned and operated outfit in Park Ridge, delivering wet hire earthmoving and civil works across the Logan region. The formula was simple: turn up on time, do the job properly, and leave the site better than you found it.

In 2026, KW | Innovations took over the business — keeping the operators, the local relationships and the work ethic, and adding investment in fleet, technology and safety systems. For clients, that means the same crew you trust, now with more capability behind it.

  • Same operators, same standardsThe people who built the reputation are still on the machines.
  • Bigger fleet, broader reachNew plant and combo capability, from residential digs to energy projects.
  • Modern systemsDigital quoting, scheduling and safety management under the KW | Innovations umbrella.
